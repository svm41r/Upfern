FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does Upfern do?

It takes a long video or stream, finds the moments worth posting, turns each into a captioned 9:16 clip, and can post those clips to your connected accounts.

What is Auto edit?

One button on any clip. It removes silences and filler words like "um" and "uh", joins what is left, and re-times the captions so they still match.

Can it post for me?

Yes. Connect YouTube, TikTok and Instagram in Settings, then post a clip to all of them in one press, or add it to the queue. Connecting needs your own developer app for each platform — the app walks you through it.

Which streams can I use?

YouTube videos and live replays, Twitch and Kick VODs, and video files you upload.

Is there a watermark?

No. Clips export clean at up to 1080p.

Do I need an AI key?

For the best picks, yes — Gemini, OpenAI, Claude, Groq and others work, or a local model through Ollama. Without one, clips are picked by loudness.
